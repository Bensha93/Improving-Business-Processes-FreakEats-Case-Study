# Improving Business Processes: FreakEats Case Study

This project focuses on analyzing, visualizing, and improving the operational workflow of **FreakEats**, an online food delivery platform. Using tools like Lucidchart, we’ll map out the existing process, identify pain points, and propose actionable improvements to enhance efficiency and user experience.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to improve a business process by:
- Understanding its current workflow.
- Identifying key stakeholders and inefficiencies.
- Visualizing the process with clear, structured flow diagrams.
- Proposing and presenting actionable improvements to optimize operations.

### **Scenario: FreakEats**
FreakEats connects customers with local restaurants and delivery partners (drivers or riders) to get food delivered quickly and efficiently. However, as with any growing platform, there are areas in its operational workflow that could be streamlined. This project aims to solve that.

---

## 🛠 The Current Workflow

The process starts with a customer placing an order and ends with the delivery of the food. Here’s the step-by-step breakdown:
1. **Order Placement**: The customer places an order through the FreakEats app.
2. **Order Acceptance**: The restaurant accepts the order and begins preparing it.
3. **Delivery Partner Assignment**: A delivery partner is assigned to pick up the order.
4. **Order Pickup**: The delivery partner picks up the order from the restaurant.
5. **Order Delivery**: The delivery partner delivers the order to the customer.

---

## 🔑 Stakeholders Involved

- **Customers**: The end-users placing food orders.
- **Restaurant Staff**: Responsible for preparing the food.
- **Delivery Partners**: Handle pickups and deliveries.
- **FreakEats Support Team**: Resolves issues and ensures customer satisfaction.

---

## 🔍 Identifying Challenges

Here are some key issues in the current process:
- **Delays in food preparation**: Restaurants may take longer than expected.
- **Delivery partner availability**: Sometimes, no delivery partner is immediately available.
- **Inefficient communication**: Delays and updated ETAs are not always communicated effectively.
- **Customer dissatisfaction**: Late deliveries impact customer trust and satisfaction.

---

## 🚀 Proposed Improvements

To tackle these challenges, the following solutions are proposed:
1. **Real-time Notifications**: Automate notifications for delivery partners and customers with accurate updates on order status.
2. **Dynamic Reassignment**: Proactively reassign delivery partners when preparation delays occur.
3. **Predictive Delivery Times**: Use historical data to improve delivery time estimates and reduce uncertainty.

---

## 🖍 Visualizing the Workflow

Using **Lucidchart**, we created a flow diagram to map the process:
1. **Start**: Customer places an order.
2. **Restaurant Prep**: The restaurant accepts and prepares the order.
3. **Decision Point**: Is a delivery partner available?
   - **Yes**: Assign a delivery partner and notify them.
   - **No**: Wait for availability.
4. **Pickup and Delivery**: Delivery partner picks up the order and delivers it to the customer.
5. **End**: The customer receives their order.
